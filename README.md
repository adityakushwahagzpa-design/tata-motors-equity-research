# Tata Motors PV – Equity Research Report (Excel Model + PDF)

A one-page equity research profile and Excel model on **Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Ltd (NSE: TMPV)**, with data up to 4 November 2026.

## Data
5 years of consolidated financials (FY22–FY26), 5 years of daily NSE prices and volume, shareholding pattern, management pay and capital structure.

## Analysis
- Margin and growth: EBITDA, EBIT, net margin, EPS, DPS
- Valuation and returns: P/E, EV/EBITDA, P/B, CFO/EBITDA, ROE, ROCE
- Market cap to enterprise value bridge
- Historical numbers adjusted for the October 2025 demerger of the commercial vehicle business

## Key findings
- EBITDA margin fell from **13.3% (FY24) to 5.6% (FY26)** after the JLR cyber incident
- Reported FY26 profit lifted by a one-time demerger gain; continuing operations loss-making
- Enterprise value **₹1.29 lakh crore**, P/B about **0.9x**

## Tools
Excel (formula-based model, live charts, source-linked data) · One-page PDF report
